## Before you start

Open this reference repository in Marpos Studio. Choose **Blog**, then open this chapter. You will make one small change, see it on the website, and save it.

## Update the page details

1. Open **Details** and find the title and description.
2. Change the description so it explains what the reader will learn. Keep it between 20 and 200 characters, as required by this reference’s content schema.
3. Leave the author and topics selected. These references supply the byline and topic links on the website.

The title appears on the chapter page and in the manual list. The description should make sense in both places.

## Edit the body

1. In the visual editor, change a sentence in the opening section.
2. Add a short paragraph or a list item. Use headings to divide tasks, rather than making a paragraph bold to look like a heading.
3. Switch to source to inspect the Markdown. Return to the visual editor to continue writing.

Markdown and Markdoc chapters support the visual workflow. The MDX example opens in the source editor to preserve its imports, components, and expressions.

## Preview, save, and reopen

1. Choose **Preview this page** and read the change in its actual layout.
2. Check the title, description, links, and hero image.
3. Save the local edit, then close and reopen the chapter to confirm it persists.
4. If you were only practising, discard your temporary changes before starting a real update.

**Check your result:** the chapter and manual list should both show the saved details. A local save does not publish the website.
