## Choose a lesson with a purpose

Open the [CMS tutorial collection](/library/?category=CMS+tutorials). Each video teaches a specific content-management task: editing with blocks, managing a media library, or adding and removing images.

These are WordPress tutorials. Their buttons and menus differ from Marpos Studio. Use them to explain a general CMS concept, then follow the Marpos-specific steps in the surrounding chapter.

## Attach a video to a chapter

1. Open a chapter in Marpos Studio’s **Blog** collection.
2. In the chapter details, find **videos**, a list of references to **Videos** records.
3. Select a lesson such as **Using the media library**. Save the chapter.
4. Open **Preview this page**. The lesson appears under **Watch & learn**, after the written instructions.
5. Press **Watch lesson** to load the external player. Use its playback and caption controls.

The reference picker stores the asset ID rather than a copied embed. That keeps the title, credit, description, and player consistent wherever the lesson is reused.

## Give the reader another way to learn

Every embedded lesson includes an **Original lesson & written guide** link. Check that link as part of review. The text instructions in the chapter should still explain the task when a reader cannot play the video.

Videos load only when the reader presses play. This avoids downloading three external players just to read a page. An internet connection is required for playback.

## Respect the source

The video record preserves its source publisher and original lesson URL. These official WordPress.org and WordPress.com lessons are embedded unchanged from YouTube. Keep the source credit with the lesson. The reference does not download or redistribute the video files.

## Insert a video inside the story

For an inline video, use **Choose video** in Studio’s story editor and select a lesson from **Videos**. Studio inserts a responsive YouTube embed. You can then adjust its size, alignment, start time, and playback options through the editor. The chapter’s `videos` reference list is an alternative for placing a reusable lesson after the written instructions.

**Check your result:** play the lesson, turn on captions, follow the written-guide link, and confirm the surrounding instructions make clear which CMS the video demonstrates.
