Content rarely lives alone. A heading sits beside navigation, a photo changes the rhythm of a page, and a summary appears in more than one place.

## Look beyond the sentence

**Preview this page** renders the current edit through the website’s own Astro templates. Studio saves the edit temporarily for rendering. Saving keeps it in the workspace; discarding restores the saved version.

Check the page at a comfortable reading width, then try a narrow window. Long titles and image captions often reveal layout problems before ordinary paragraphs do.

| Check | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Title | A clear opening that wraps comfortably |
| Image | A useful crop and descriptive alternative text |
| Links | The right destination and meaningful link text |
| Story | Headings that help a reader scan |

## Browse the surrounding website

Use **Browse site** to inspect the saved website separately. Return to the manual list to check the summary and date, and open the contributor link to confirm the byline.
